01 / THE WORD

Before it became a name, it had a meaning.

Dravya (द्रव्य) is a Sanskrit word associated with substance, entity and existence.

In the Indian philosophical tradition of Vaisheshika, dravya is understood as one of the fundamental categories of reality. It refers to substance — that in which qualities and actions exist.

In Ayurveda, the word also describes substances and materials used in medicinal preparations.

Different traditions. Different contexts. A recurring concern with the nature of what exists.

03 / THE FIELD

Reality, described through fields.

Modern physics does not describe the universe simply as a collection of tiny objects moving through empty space.

In quantum field theory, fields are fundamental ingredients of the description of nature. They extend throughout space and time and interact with one another.

The electron is associated with an electron field. The photon with the electromagnetic field. Quarks with their respective fields.

What we identify as a particle can be understood as a quantised excitation of a corresponding field.

Not a miniature object hidden inside something larger. A different way of describing the fundamental structure of the physical world.

A NOTE ON THE PHYSICS

Quantum field theory is a mathematical framework, not simply a metaphor about interconnectedness. The analogy explored by DRAVYA later on is philosophical, not a claim that human interests are quantum fields.

04 / THE GROUND STATE

The absence of particles is not the absence of fields.

A quantum field can exist in a state containing no particle excitations.

Its lowest-energy state is known as the ground state.

Even this state is governed by quantum mechanics. For example, a quantum harmonic oscillator retains zero-point energy.

The quantum vacuum, therefore, is not classical empty space. It is the lowest-energy state of the fields.

Nothing in the everyday sense. Not nothing in the physical description.

06 / EXPRESSION

A change of state.

Interactions can change the state of a quantum system.

Energy can be transferred. Excitations can be created or destroyed. Systems can transition between different configurations.

Excitation is not necessarily permanent. A system can evolve, interact and transition towards lower-energy states.

Physics describes these processes through states, dynamics, energy and interaction.

DRAVYA takes a different question from this:

What if the different expressions of something are not separate from what it fundamentally is?

08 / THE CREATOR

It began with a filmmaker.

There was no elaborate plan. No carefully constructed career. No grand theory about the future.

There was simply an interest in making films.

But filmmaking was never just about putting images together.

A film needed a story. A story needed characters, places, landscapes, events, time and sound.

The filmmaker became interested in storytelling.

Storytelling led to writing. Writing led to worlds. Worlds led to sound, music, composition and design.

Then came a larger question.

What is the story of everything?

The story of people. The story of our species. The story of Earth. The story of the universe.

Before attempting to tell that story, there was something to understand.

Existence. Matter. Space. Time. The origin and structure of reality.

Philosophy opened one door. Physics opened another.

Eventually came a master's degree in physics, and with it, an encounter with mathematical frameworks, quantum mechanics, nuclear physics and particle physics.

Questions that once belonged to stories now had equations, experiments and mathematical descriptions.

But something had remained unchanged.

The filmmaker was still there. The storyteller was still there. The interest in sound, art, writing and science had not disappeared.

Physics had not replaced the filmmaker.

The filmmaker had not replaced the storyteller.

None of them had cancelled the others.

10 / THE UNDERLYING FIELD

One person. Many expressions.

A person is never just one thing.

Interests change. Skills develop. Contradictions emerge. New questions appear. Old obsessions return.

A song, a scientific equation, a film, a fictional world, a landscape, a discovery or a question can all emerge from the same individual.

They are different expressions, but they do not necessarily represent different identities.

The underlying person remains. The expressions evolve.
