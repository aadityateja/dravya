01 / The word

Before DRAVYA was a project, it was a word.

Dravya comes from the Sanskrit dravya (द्रव्य), a word used to refer to substance, entity, or that which exists.

Across Indian philosophical traditions, the word has carried different but related meanings. In Vaisheshika , dravya refers to substance as one of the fundamental categories through which reality can be understood.

In Ayurveda, the word can refer to a substance used medicinally — a drug, material, or preparation used to treat disease, preserve health, or produce an effect.

03 / The field

Everything begins with a field.

In modern physics, a field is a physical quantity defined throughout space and time. In quantum field theory, the description goes deeper.

The fundamental objects of the Standard Model are described by quantum fields. What we call a particle can be understood as a quantized excitation of its corresponding field.

There is a quark field. A photon field. An electron field. And other quantum fields. The particle is not simply a tiny classical object sitting somewhere inside the field. It is an excitation of the field.

This page deliberately keeps the physics at the conceptual level. DRAVYA has a separate field dedicated to exploring quantum field theory, particles, excitations, vacuum states, fluctuations and perturbations in much greater depth.

04 / Ground state

Even nothing is not nothing.

A quantum field does not simply disappear when there are no particles present. Its lowest-energy configuration is called its ground state.

Quantum mechanics prevents a quantum harmonic oscillator from possessing exactly zero energy. Even the ground state carries non-zero zero-point energy.

This is why the quantum vacuum is not simply an absolute emptiness. The vacuum is the lowest-energy state of the quantum fields — not the absence of the fields themselves.

05 / Perturbation

Introduce a disturbance.

The field responds.

It fluctuates. It becomes excited. It changes state.

Something emerges.

06 / Return

Excitation is temporary.

An excited quantum system can transition toward lower-energy states. When energy is released, the system can move toward a more stable configuration.

In the language of physics, this is about states, energy and interactions. In the language of DRAVYA, we imagine something beautifully familiar:

everything is always becoming.

A disturbance happens. A response follows. An expression appears. And eventually, the system changes again.

08 / The creator

The creator of DRAVYA once wanted only one thing: to become a filmmaker.

As a child, there was no elaborate career plan. No map. No carefully constructed destination. There was simply a dream — to make films.

But there was a problem. He did not yet know how to make one.

Slowly, the idea of filmmaking began to expand. Films were not merely moving images. They were stories. Stories needed characters. Places. Landscapes. Events. Time. Sound. Silence. Music.

So the filmmaker slowly became a storyteller.

And storytelling opened another door. A story could be told through sound.

So came sound design. Music recording. Music composition. Writing.

Then came another question:

What exactly is a story?

And if stories are about people, then what is the story of people?

What is the story of our species? What is the story of Earth? What is the story of the universe?

And suddenly the question became much larger than filmmaking.

Before trying to tell a story about the universe, perhaps he needed to understand the universe itself.

Philosophy appeared. Existence. Time. Matter. Space. The origin of everything.

And eventually, fundamental physics.

He entered the world of physics through a master's degree and found himself immersed in ideas that were strangely close to the questions he had been asking all along.

Mathematical frameworks. Quantum mechanics. Nuclear physics. Particle physics. The structure of matter. The fields beneath what we call reality.

The filmmaker had not disappeared. The storyteller had not disappeared. The physicist had not replaced them.

They were all still there.

10 / DRAVYA

One underlying field. Many expressions.

Human thoughts, passions, hobbies, contradictions, memories, failures, obsessions, discoveries and experiences can appear to be completely different things.

But perhaps they can also be understood as different expressions of the same underlying person.

Just as different quantum fields can produce different excitations, the different parts of a human life can be understood as expressions emerging from something deeper.

A song. A scientific equation. A film. A landscape. A fictional character. A scientific question. A memory. A contradiction. A particle. A story.

Different excitations.

One field.
