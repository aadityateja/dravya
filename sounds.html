A FIELD PERTURBED.

In physics, a perturbation is a disturbance introduced into a system.

Something changes. The field responds. A new state emerges.

PERTURBATION begins with this idea and translates it into sound. Rather than treating each track as an isolated song, the EP explores four different states of the same field.

The disturbance is not the end of the system. It is what allows the system to reveal something different.
