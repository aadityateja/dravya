Every discovery begins somewhere inside an enormous structure of things already known.

Physics is not a collection of equations to memorize. It is a network of relationships that can be reconstructed.

This is a map of those relationships.

If you call yourself a physicist, how much of this can you derive without looking?

The deeper the map goes, the less the equations look like isolated formulas. Classical mechanics becomes a language of symmetries. Quantum mechanics becomes amplitudes. Relativity becomes geometry. Quantum field theory turns particles into excitations of fields. And nuclear physics becomes the place where these ideas meet matter, energy, interaction and experiment.
