THE STORY

THE EYE
OF THE BIRD

The forenoon breeze moved gently through the great trees of the Gurukul at Hastinapur.

Somewhere among the rustling leaves, arrows were already flying.

The archery class was underway under the watchful eye of Acharya Drona.

Drona, a masterful teacher, trained the young Pandavas and Kauravas each day with discipline and rigour.

Today's objective was simple.

A wooden bird had been placed upon a branch.

Hit it.

The young students took their positions.

They learned the posture, the grip, the tension of the bow and the discipline required before an arrow could ever be released.

The morning slowly brightened over Hastinapur.

Yudhishthira stood first.

"What do you see?"
"I can see you, Acharya. I can see my brothers, the trees and the birds. I am waiting for the right moment to aim."

Drona nodded.

Then Duryodhana stepped forward.

"Acharya, I can see the whole forest."

Drona knew.

Nakula came next.

"I can see the bird upon the branch."

Drona smiled.

Finally came Bhima.

He looked toward the tree.

"I can see the apple over there."

For a moment, the seriousness of the lesson disappeared.

Finally, Drona turned toward Arjuna.

Arjuna was still as thunder.

His eyes were fixed.

There was no sign of movement.

His posture was perfect.

DRONA ASKED
"What do you see?"

What came as a reply sent a chill through Drona.

Arjuna did not move.

His eyes remained fixed upon the target.

"I see nothing but
the eye of the bird."

Drona's eyes lit up.

"Marvelous."

"Go for it!"
→

Like lightning, the arrow left the bow.

For a fraction of a second, everything seemed to freeze.

The arrow travelled straight toward the wooden bird.

And struck its eye perfectly.

Arjuna lowered his bow.

He looked toward his teacher and smiled like a child.

THE STORY ENDS HERE.
INTERPRETATION

WHAT LIES
BENEATH?

Although we never know if this event really occurred somewhere in the past on this planet, what is important is the deeper meaning hidden within the story.

It speaks of focus.

Of excluding everything that does not matter and identifying exactly what does.

THE BIRD → THE GOAL THE EYE → THE DEFINITION OF THE GOAL THE FOREST → THE DISTRACTIONS THE BREEZE → EVERYTHING THAT MOVES AROUND US DRONA → THE INNER CRITIC ARJUNA → YOU

The bird represents a goal.

But the bird's eye is more than a goal. It is the precise definition of that goal.

The forest, the breeze, the trees and everything surrounding it become distractions.

Drona becomes the inner critic — the voice that asks the question:

What exactly are you looking at?

And Arjuna becomes you.

Success becomes almost inevitable when you know exactly what you want, define it clearly and aim with the exclusion of everything else.

PHYSICS

THE BOW
& THE ARROW

01

Energy

Drawing the bow requires work. That energy becomes stored within the deformed bow and string, waiting to be released.

02

Elasticity

The bow bends under force and attempts to return toward its original shape. Its geometry and material determine how it stores and releases mechanical energy.

03

Motion

When the string is released, energy is transferred to the arrow, accelerating it along its trajectory.

04

Vibration

The release does not bring the entire system to rest. The string and bow continue to oscillate, while energy is distributed through motion, sound and deformation.

05

Materials

The behaviour of a bow depends strongly on the materials from which it is made. Strength, stiffness, elasticity and internal structure all influence its performance.

06

The Arrow

The arrow's mass, geometry, aerodynamic stability and interaction with the surrounding air determine how it travels through space.
