THE CONCEPT

A landscape is not merely a place.

It is a system of relationships. Geometry becomes terrain. Light becomes atmosphere. Empty space becomes distance. Every surface describes something about the world that contains it.

This landscape is an experiment in constructing a world from simple forms and allowing the observer to decide how that world should be understood.

The model is not a photograph of a place. It is an interpretation of one — a small environment where physical space becomes a medium for imagination.
